Fill

Bucket fill that copes with noisy AI pictures: it fills every close shade, not only the exact colour. Or fill a whole background in one tap.

Choose a picture, or try the sample.

To save: press and hold the picture, then choose Download image. On a computer, right-click it and choose Save image as.

Sprite Tools version 0.1.0. GNU GPL v3 or later. This page makes no network requests: the picture never leaves your device. The same method as the Python tool; source and tests at github.com/tfrancovitch/pixeltools.
