Pixel Snap

Finds the real pixel grid in AI "pixel art", rebuilds it so one visible pixel is one real pixel, and sorts a batch by what you need. Everything runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Pick one picture or a whole batch. On a computer you can also drop pictures here.
